Muscle readiness

Recovery improves automatically with time after a logged workout.

How it works

The app records the muscles you actually train. A muscle is heavily fatigued immediately afterwards, then becomes more available over roughly 2–3 days.

The generator favours fresher muscle groups, but your manual selection always wins.

This is a training-planning model, not a medical measure of recovery. Pain or injury should override the score.
